Hello! I am a Computer Science PhD student in the [AIMM Lab](https://aimm.epfl.ch/) at EPFL supervised by [Charlotte Bunne](https://people.epfl.ch/charlotte.bunne?lang=en). Before joining EPFL, I completed my bachelor's and master's degrees in Mathematics at ETH Zurich, where I was awarded the Willi Studer Prize for my master's degree and the ETH Medal for my master's thesis. I was also a visiting researcher at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, where I worked with Gad Getz and David Sontag.

My research focuses on **methods for representation learning and generative modeling**, and on their **application to spatial omics data**. At the moment, I am particularly interested in (i) methods that bridge heterogeneous assays, which typically occur in omics data, and (ii) in the modeling of temporal dynamics.
